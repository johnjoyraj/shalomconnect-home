About the Platform

About Shalom Connect

Shalom Connect is a central digital platform connecting multiple independently operated digital projects under one clear, trustworthy web address.

Rather than merging unrelated tools into a single system, each application keeps its own dedicated database, authentication, security rules, and administration—giving users reliable, focused experiences across community, commerce, education, and business operations.

Independent Architecture

Each subdomain hosts its own standalone application with dedicated data isolation and access control.

Unified Navigation

One memorable gateway at shalomconnect.in to reach all public and administrative platforms cleanly.

Privacy & Security First

HTTPS across all domains, strict separation of public directories and private operational portals.
